# Mila 1.9.6

**Fixes**

- **Meeting recordings keep the other side of the call again.** Only the first meeting or app-audio recording after opening Mila captured the call's audio. Every later recording in that session got your microphone alone, so the other participants were missing from the transcript, and "App audio" recordings came out empty and were rejected as too quiet. Quitting and reopening Mila was the only way to get the next recording right. Each recording now gets its own app-audio capture, so every meeting you record is complete, however many you record without relaunching.
